It stays on this device unless you save and send it. Test mode also adds a device self-test and a synthetic performance at the bottom of the Practice page.

    Calibrate the microphone

    Tune your instrument first. The app then listens to the room for a few seconds and to each open string, and adjusts to this microphone, room and instrument. Calibrate again if you change device, microphone or room.

      Everything happens in your browser – no sound or data is sent anywhere. Pitches are measured in equal temperament against the A you set; notes tuned as pure fifths can read a few cents away from equal temperament.

      Install Scale Practice

      As an app it opens full-screen from your home screen or desktop, and works without a connection.

      On iPhone and iPad, in Safari:

      1. Tap the Share button (□ with an arrow up) – at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, at the top on iPad.
      2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
      3. Tap Add. Scale Practice is now on your home screen.

      In Chrome on iPhone, the Share button is at the top right, next to the address.

      Open this page in Chrome or Edge (or Safari on iPhone and iPad) to install it. On Android, the browser menu ⋮ also has Install app or Add to Home screen.

      ✓ You are using the installed app.

      Your syllabus, results and settings are kept on each device. On iPhone and iPad the installed app keeps its own copy, separate from Safari: use Export everything in Config in Safari and Import everything in the app to bring them across.
